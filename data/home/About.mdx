I am an HCI researcher working at the intersection of design for older adults, human-AI interaction, and humanistic design for wellbeing. My research investigates how interaction design can support dignity, agency, and meaning-making when people face displacement — by aging, by life transition events like retirement, by accelerating technological change, or by the rise of increasingly autonomous AI. 

In my master's, I designed an AI future-self system for recently retired individuals, exploring how imagining the future with AI-generated future self materials can support anticipatory meaning-making around later-life transitions. 

Continuing my studies in the Ph.D. program, I am currently extending this inquiry across three connected sites: positive aging as a lifespan concern (not only for older adults but also for younger people) and the design of human agency in a near-future shaped by agentic AI. Across these threads, I pursue design approaches that move beyond mitigating loss toward enabling self-reflection — reframing displacement as a generative occasion for growth.
